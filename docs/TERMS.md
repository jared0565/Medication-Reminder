## 2. Reminders can fail

Reminders depend on your device, its operating system, its notification
settings, your network, your browser vendor's push infrastructure, and this
service. Any of these can fail, and a reminder may be **late, duplicated, or not
delivered at all**.

This is stated as a plain fact rather than buried: a reminder that does not
arrive looks exactly like a day with nothing scheduled. Keep an independent way
of tracking anything that matters clinically.

## 4. Your data

You own what you enter. We store your schedule encrypted and cannot read it (see

Because the schedule is encrypted with a key only your devices hold, **we cannot
recover it for you** if you lose every paired device. That is a deliberate
trade: it is the same property that stops us, or anyone who compels us, from
reading it.
